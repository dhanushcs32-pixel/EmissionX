================================================================================
                    EMISSIONX: CARBON FOOTPRINT INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM
                              PROJECT SUMMARY & CAPABILITIES
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EmissionX is an end-to-end Computer Vision (CV) and IoT sensor fusion platform
engineered to monitor, quantify, and simulate real-time carbon emissions and
decarbonization strategies across two primary sectors:
  - Pillar 1: Vehicular & Urban Mobility Emissions (Fuel stations & traffic queues)
  - Pillar 2: Industrial Point-Source Smokestacks (Factories, boilers & CEMS)

The system unifies real-time computer vision inference, empirical emission
physics, IoT sensor telemetry, carbon credit valuation (INR), and AI-driven
regulatory compliance advisories under a unified FastAPI backend.


================================================================================
2. WHAT THE SYSTEM CAN DO (CORE CAPABILITIES)
================================================================================

[PILLAR 1: VEHICULAR & URBAN MOBILITY INTELLIGENCE]
---------------------------------------------------
* Multi-Class Vehicle Detection & Tracking:
  - Powered by YOLO11n object detection + ByteTrack multi-object tracker.
  - Detects, classifies, and tracks cars, motorcycles, auto-rickshaws, buses, and trucks.
  - Maintains persistent tracking IDs and trajectory history across video frames.

* Automatic License Plate Recognition (ALPR / LPR):
  - Integrates EasyOCR with CLAHE contrast enhancement and bilateral filtering.
  - Extracts and normalizes vehicle registration plates at regular tracking intervals.

* Queue Dwell Time & Idling Wastage Analysis:
  - Monitors fuel dispensing bay occupancy and queue waiting times.
  - Converts excess idle time into wasted fuel and avoidable tailpipe CO2 emissions.

* Decarbonization Policy Simulation:
  - Simulates emission impacts of:
    1. EV adoption rate (%)
    2. E20 Bioethanol fuel blending (%)
    3. Queue flow / ANPR bay optimization (%)
    4. Heavy commercial BS-III / BS-IV vehicle bans

---

[PILLAR 2: INDUSTRIAL SMOKESTACK INTELLIGENCE]
----------------------------------------------
* Dual-Mode Plume Detection:
  - Primary Mode: Deep-learning YOLO plume weights (models/smoke.pt) for smoke plumes.
  - Zero-Weight Fallback Mode: OpenCV MOG2 background subtractor with morphological
    filtering and ROI intensity analysis (enables execution on low-spec CPUs).

* Optical Ringelmann Opacity Indexing:
  - Calculates plume opacity (0.0% to 100.0%) from grayscale pixel brightness.
  - Maps plume density directly into standardized Ringelmann levels (0 to 5):
    - Level 0-1 (0-20%): Normal operation / clean steam (1.0x baseline multiplier)
    - Level 2-3 (21-60%): Incomplete combustion warning (1.25x penalty multiplier)
    - Level 4-5 (>60%): Black carbon violation / scrubber failure (1.60x penalty)

* CEMS (Continuous Emission Monitoring System) IoT Synchronization:
  - Synthesizes and couples physical boiler sensor metrics with visual plume opacity:
    - Fuel burn rate (e.g., Coal @ ~1200 kg/hr with opacity-correlated surges)
    - Flue gas exit temperature (160°C to 240°C)
    - Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) concentration (80 to 350 ppm)
    - Continuous audit and diagnostic telemetry logging

* Industrial Decarbonization Policy Simulation:
  - Simulates mitigation scenarios including:
    1. Electrostatic Precipitator (ESP) / Wet Scrubber efficiency (0% to 99%)
    2. Primary combustion stoichiometric air/fuel damper tuning
    3. Fuel switching (Coal -> Natural Gas, Furnace Oil, or Biomass co-firing)

---

[UNIFIED BACKEND & PLATFORM SERVICES]
-------------------------------------
* Ambient Environmental Ingestion:
  - Real-time weather sync from Open-Meteo API (temperature, wind speed, wind
    heading, AQI) with in-memory caching and offline fallback.

* Carbon Economics Engine:
  - Translates avoided CO2 emissions into monetary savings in Indian Rupees (INR ₹)
    under the Indian Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS ~₹1,350/tCO2e).

* GenAI Sustainability Copilot:
  - Generates telemetry-grounded regulatory guidance under CPCB / KSPCB standards,
    Air Act 1981 compliance checks, ESG score deltas, and CapEx ROI timelines.

* Synthetic Factory Video Generator:
  - Automatically synthesizes multi-phase smokestack CCTV video for test suites
    if no physical camera feed is connected.


================================================================================
3. ALL OUTPUTS PRODUCED BY THE SYSTEM
================================================================================

A. PROCESSED VIDEO OUTPUTS
--------------------------
1. cache/factory_annotated.mp4
   - Industrial video overlay featuring glowing neon bounding boxes color-coded
     by Ringelmann severity (Green, Amber, Crimson Red).
   - Glassmorphism tactical HUD showing real-time opacity %, fuel burn rate,
     CO2 emission rate (kg/hr), and CEMS diagnostic status.

2. backend/data/annotated_fuel_station.mp4
   - Vehicular CCTV output annotated with ByteTrack IDs, vehicle class badges,
     and recognized license plate numbers.

3. factory_stack.mp4 (Synthetic Generator)
   - Procedurally rendered industrial CCTV footage simulating clean steam,
     incomplete combustion, and black carbon violation phases.


B. STRUCTURED TELEMETRY & DATA ARTIFACTS
----------------------------------------
1. cache/factory_telemetry.json
   - Frame-by-frame industrial telemetry conforming to strict Pydantic schemas:
     * Node identifier & sector metadata
     * Ambient weather & AQI
     * Optical smoke opacity % & Ringelmann level
     * CEMS metrics (fuel burn rate, flue gas temp, SO2 ppm)
     * Real-time baseline CO2 vs. waste CO2 (kg/hr)
     * Audit log streams

2. backend/data/phase1_summary.json
   - Vehicle tracking dataset including:
     * Total unique vehicles detected
     * Class breakdown (cars, bikes, buses, trucks)
     * Video metadata (dimensions, FPS, duration)
     * Individual per-vehicle records (track ID, timestamps, frame count, plates)


C. REST API ENDPOINTS & LIVE RESPONSES (FastAPI on Port 8000)
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. GET /api/telemetry?sector=industrial|vehicular|all
   - Provides continuous, circular real-time telemetry stream for UI dashboards.

2. POST /api/simulate
   - Runs deterministic carbon reduction calculations and returns:
     * Baseline vs. Projected CO2 (kg/hr)
     * Total avoided CO2 & net reduction percentage (%)
     * Hourly and Annual financial savings in INR (₹)

3. POST /api/ai-advice
   - Returns structured advisories with immediate engineering action items,
     regulatory status (CPCB/KSPCB/MoRTH), and ESG reporting impacts.

4. GET /api/environment
   - Returns live ambient weather parameters (temperature, wind, AQI).

5. GET /api/video/{sector}
   - Streams annotated video files (factory_annotated.mp4 or fuel_annotated.mp4)
     for direct web browser display.

6. GET /api/health
   - System health, cache diagnostic counters, and module status check.
