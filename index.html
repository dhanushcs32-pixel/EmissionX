01Computer vision pipeline

Every vehicle. Every gram.

YOLO tracks cars, trucks, buses and bikes across fuel station forecourts. Each vehicle gets a class, a dwell time, a fuel estimate and a CO₂ number — all from one camera.

02Industrial smoke opacity

Ringelmann in real time.

A second camera watches the factory stack. Background subtraction isolates the plume, grayscale intensity gives the opacity, and the Ringelmann grade sets the emission multiplier.
